Positive feedback loop: Doing well gives you more time, and more time makes it easier to keep doing well.
The loop:
1. Collect a clock -> global.timer += 15 * 60, so you gain 15 seconds and see the "+15s" popup.
2. More time on the clock -> you're under less pressure, so you can slow down, think through the puzzle and coordinate with your partner.
3. Less pressure -> you can take detours and try risky jumps to reach the next clock. Losing a few seconds to an attempt no longer ends the run.
4. Another clock -> even more time → back to step 2.

The negative feedback loop: the rescue clock
A struggling player gets help, and that help makes them struggle less.
The loop:
1. The player dies. They go back to their checkpoint and lose about 1 second of respawn time, plus the progress they'd made.
2. The timer keeps dropping -> more pressure -> rushed jumps -> more deaths. Left alone, this would spiral downwards.
3. Once they've died 3 times and have under 30 seconds left, a rescue clock appears just above their respawn point. They grab it: +15 seconds.
4. More time -> less pressure -> they can slow down and make the jump carefully → fewer deaths → the spiral stops.
